RESEARCH PROJECT · PORT HARCOURT, NIGERIA

Does weather affect your phone signal?

A year of daily weather data — temperature, humidity, pressure, wind — compared against MTN and Airtel signal strength in Port Harcourt, to find out how much of your signal quality actually comes down to the sky.

The question

Nigerian networks are known to weaken during storms and heavy rain, but how much of that is really weather, and how much is something else — congestion, distance to a tower, terrain? This project uses 365 days of matched weather and signal-strength readings to measure it directly, then builds a model that can estimate signal strength from live weather conditions anywhere.
