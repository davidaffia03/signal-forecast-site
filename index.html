Weather and GSM signal strength in Port Harcourt

By David Affia. Data from a PhD research project on weather and GSM signal strength, Port Harcourt, 2025.

I compared a year of daily weather readings (temperature, humidity, pressure and wind speed) with MTN and Airtel signal strength in Port Harcourt. The aim was to measure how much of the change in signal can be linked to the weather.

People here often notice the network getting worse during storms and heavy rain. Weather is only one possible cause. Congestion, distance from the tower and terrain also matter, and this dataset says nothing about them. So this project measures the weather part only.
